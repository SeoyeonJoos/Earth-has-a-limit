Solutions and Call to Action

Proposed Solutions

  • Reduce single-use plastics and practice proper waste segregation.
  • Protect and restore forests and other natural habitats.
  • Support responsible, sustainable consumption.
  • Promote environmental education in schools and communities.

Ways People Can Help

  • Bring reusable bags, bottles, and containers.
  • Segregate and properly dispose of waste.
  • Conserve water and energy at home.
  • Share verified information about conservation with others.
