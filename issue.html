About the Issue

Description of the Issue

Human activities are putting growing pressure on forests, oceans, wildlife, and other natural resources. Habitat destruction, pollution, unsustainable consumption, and climate change threaten the ecosystems that provide food, clean water, and a stable climate.

Causes and Effects

Causes

  • Habitat destruction
  • Pollution and plastic waste
  • Unsustainable resource consumption
  • Climate change

Effects

  • Declining wildlife populations
  • Threatened species
  • Loss of forests and natural habitats
  • Pressure on food, water, and climate stability

The Reality in Numbers

73%
Wildlife Population Decline

The average size of monitored wildlife populations declined by 73% globally between 1970 and 2022.

Source: WWF, Living Planet Report 2026.
1,106
Threatened Fauna Species

The Philippines recorded 1,106 threatened fauna species in 2023.

Source: Philippine Statistics Authority, Compendium of Philippine Environment Statistics.
2.7 Million Tons
Plastic Waste

An estimated 2.7 million tons of plastic waste are generated annually in the Philippines.

Source: World Bank.
7.23 Million Hectares
Forest Area

The Philippines had approximately 7.23 million hectares of forest area in 2020.

Source: Philippine Statistics Authority.
